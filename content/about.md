I'm Rick Waterman, a Lead Cloud Architect in the Vancouver, WA / Portland, OR
metro. I've spent ten-plus years in backend and cloud engineering — leading
architecture and delivery for AWS serverless systems, event-driven services,
and company-wide data platforms, and before that building full-stack systems
for payment-risk detection at scale, financial research tooling, and
agricultural IoT.

This blog is where longer-form writing lands. Working references live at
[notes.rickwaterman.com](https://notes.rickwaterman.com), and the main site
with my resume is [rickwaterman.com](https://rickwaterman.com).
